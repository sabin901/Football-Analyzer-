# Asset credits

League marks identify the competitions being analyzed. They do not imply affiliation, endorsement or ownership.

| Competition | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Premier League | ESPN league identity artwork |
| La Liga | ESPN league identity artwork |

Exact URLs and retrieval dates are recorded in `frontend/assets/leagues/sources.json` in the repository, also published at `assets/leagues/sources.json`. Assets are bundled locally, preserve their aspect ratio and have a text fallback. Club crests and player portraits come from ESPN; kits may reflect an older club. The StatsBomb mark attributes historical event data.

Interface icons are vendored Lucide SVG paths. Their license is retained in `docs/LUCIDE_LICENSE.txt`. The pitch and charts are application SVG, not stock photographs or fictional player imagery.
